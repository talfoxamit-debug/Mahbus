Mahbus  ·  محبوس

The classic Middle-Eastern game of "the imprisoned" — a cousin of backgammon.

The idea. Both players start with all 15 pieces stacked in opposite far corners and race them home in opposite directions, then bear them off the board. First to bear off all 15 wins.

The twist (mahbus = "imprisoned"). There is no bar. If you land a piece on a single enemy piece, you sit on top and trap it. A trapped piece is frozen and cannot move until you lift your piece(s) off it. A point held by 2 or more enemy pieces is blocked — you cannot land there.

On your turn. Roll the dice and move one piece per die (doubles give you four moves). Click a piece to see its legal moves highlighted, then click where to go.
